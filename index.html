This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Tasbeeh Counter itself does not collect any personal information. All your Tasbeeh data, Dhikr preferences, and settings remain on your device and are never transmitted to any server we control.

However, our app displays advertisements through Unity Ads, a third-party advertising network. Unity Ads collects certain data as described in Section 3 below.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your Tasbeeh counting history
  • Your app preferences (language, theme, counter style, sound settings)
  • Your haptic feedback preferences

This data is stored using SharedPreferences and never leaves your device.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Data Collection

Our app uses Unity Ads to display advertisements. Unity Ads may collect the following data to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud:

  • Device identifiers (including Android Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • App interaction data (ad impressions, clicks)
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)

This data is collected and processed by Unity Ads. For more information, please review Unity's Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

4. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for general audiences age 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Internet: To load ads and app content
  • Network state: To check ad availability
  • Advertising ID: Required by Unity Ads for Android 13+

We do NOT request access to your camera, contacts, photos, storage, or precise location.

6. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data on any server we control, there is nothing to access, modify, or delete on our end.

For data collected by Unity Ads, you can exercise your rights (opt-out, deletion requests) directly through Unity's privacy channels and your device's advertising settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app with an updated "Last updated" date.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Tasbeeh Counter: Dhikr & Dua app ("the App"), developed by Ali Hussein. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

The App itself does not collect personal information directly. However, the App uses Unity Ads, a third-party advertising SDK, which may collect the following data:

  • Device identifiers (including Android Advertising ID)
  • Coarse location (derived from IP address, not GPS)
  • App usage and interaction data
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language)

2. How We Use Information

The information collected by Unity Ads is used solely to:

  • Serve relevant advertisements
  • Measure ad performance
  • Improve the advertising experience

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses Unity Ads. For details on how Unity Ads collects and processes data, please review Unity's privacy policy:

https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

4. Data Stored on Your Device

Your Tasbeeh count history and app settings (language, theme, counter style, sound preferences) are stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to this data, and it is never transmitted to our servers.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the App, please contact us.

6. Advertising ID Opt-Out

You can reset or opt out of interest-based advertising at any time through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization"

7. Data Security

We do not store or transmit any personal data from the App. All user data (Tasbeeh counts, preferences) remains on your device and is not accessible to us.

8. Your Rights

You may uninstall the App at any time, which will remove all locally stored data. For requests related to data collected by Unity Ads, please refer to Unity's privacy policy linked above.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
